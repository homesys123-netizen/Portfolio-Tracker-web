Property estimates

The automatic estimate of a property is only an indication, it is not a valuation or an appraisal. It shows what the property would be worth today if its price had risen since you bought it at the average rate of homes in its country or area, according to published house price statistics. It does not know the actual property: the real value depends on its condition, age, location, improvements and the local market, among other things. The automatic estimate may differ considerably from the real market value of your property. For a formal valuation, ask a qualified real estate appraiser in your area. Your own estimate, if you enter one, takes precedence over the automatic one. Renovations dated after your estimate are added to it. The estimate is included in the total value of your portfolio and in the profit and return the app shows, so these figures are only indications as well.

Data sources

The house price statistics behind the automatic estimate of a property come from public sources. For the whole country: Eurostat (house price index, quarterly data; reused under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence) and the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) (residential property price statistics, BIS Data Portal). If you enter a postal code, the estimate can follow your region where an office publishes prices for it:

  • United States: Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA), House Price Index by state. Source: FHFA HPI® (all-transactions index, quarterly, by state, not seasonally adjusted).
  • United Kingdom: HM Land Registry, UK House Price Index by region. Contains HM Land Registry data © Crown copyright and database right. This data is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right; Contains Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right; Contains National Statistics data © Crown copyright and database right (for the assignment of postcodes to regions).
  • Spain: Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), housing price index by autonomous community. Elaboración propia con datos extraídos del sitio web del INE: www.ine.es. The INE does not take part in, sponsor or endorse the app.
  • Netherlands: Statistics Netherlands (CBS), price index of existing owner-occupied homes by province and for Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague and Utrecht.
  • France: Insee, Notaires-Insee price index of second-hand dwellings (Paris, the departments of the Paris region, Marseille, Lyon and the rest of the provinces); source: Notaires de France, Insee.
  • Ireland: Central Statistics Office (CSO), Residential Property Price Index (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0).
  • Poland: Statistics Poland (GUS), median price per square metre of dwellings sold, by voivodeship; it is used only to correct the national index for the region.
  • Norway: Statistics Norway (SSB), price index for existing dwellings by region (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0). Source: Statistics Norway.
  • Denmark: Statistics Denmark (DST), price index for sales of property, one-family houses, by region. Source: Statistics Denmark, StatBank.dk, table EJ56.
  • Sweden: Statistics Sweden (SCB), real estate price index for one- and two-dwelling buildings for permanent living, by area. Source: Statistics Sweden.
  • Slovenia: Statistical Office of the Republic of Slovenia (SURS), house price indices of existing flats (SiStat, table 0419001S): Ljubljana, Maribor and the rest of the country. Source: SURS.
  • Belgium: Statbel (Statistics Belgium), house price index of existing dwellings by region (Brussels, Flanders, Wallonia), under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Source: Statbel. The index of earlier years is worked out by Portfolio Tracker from the published year-on-year changes, so it is our calculation and may differ slightly from the figures of Statbel.

The lists that assign a postal code to a region are made from the postal code lists of GeoNames (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0), the Eircode routing key list of An Post and the public data named above. The estimate itself is calculated by Portfolio Tracker from these statistics, the purchase price and exchange rates: it is our own calculation and not a statistic of any of these offices. Where a region has no data, or no postal code is given, the index of the whole country is used.
