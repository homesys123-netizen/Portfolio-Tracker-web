All your investments in one place

Portfolio Tracker follows your stocks, ETFs, crypto assets, precious metals and real estate, and the cash you hold, in one clear overview. Your portfolio stays on your phone.

  • Total value and allocation at a glance, shown in 11 currencies
  • Profit and loss, and the value of your portfolio over time
  • Analytics: compare the performance of your portfolio or any asset you own with any ETF, share or coin and see how they did over time, and which of your holdings earned the most
  • Honest return figures: total profit, annual return (IRR) and the return without your deposits (TWR), with a short explanation of each
  • Price charts of your shares, ETFs, crypto assets and metals
  • Real estate: follow what your property is worth, with an automatic estimate from official house price statistics (by region from your postal code in selected countries) or your own estimate, add renovations, and see your net worth after mortgages
  • Mortgage and rental analysis: monthly payment, extra payments and the interest they save, rental yield and return on your equity
  • Dividends of your stocks and ETFs can be added to your cash automatically, based on your preference
  • Import your trades from a spreadsheet (CSV), with a template to fill in
  • Markets: follow the main stock markets, crypto coins and bond yields, and see which stocks and coins moved the most today
  • Savings plans: set up monthly purchases that are added to your holdings automatically
  • Backup and restore of your data
  • English, German, French, Italian and Spanish

Your data stays yours

Your portfolio, your trades and every figure of the analytics are stored and calculated on your phone. Nothing is uploaded: there is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no tracking. When you compare with an ETF or a share, the app only asks a public data provider for the price history of that symbol, never for your amounts. Read the Privacy Policy.

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Free to use.

The app is in testing and is not in the app stores yet.

Not investment advice

Portfolio Tracker is a tool for your own record keeping. Prices and figures come from third parties, may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate, and are for information only. Nothing in the app is investment, tax or legal advice.
