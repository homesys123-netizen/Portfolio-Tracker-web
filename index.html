All your investments in one place

Portfolio Tracker follows your stocks, ETFs, crypto assets and precious metals, and the cash you hold, in one clear overview. Your portfolio stays on your phone.

  • Total value and allocation at a glance, in euro or US dollar
  • Profit and loss, and the value of your portfolio over time
  • Markets: indices, bond yields and the biggest movers of the day
  • Recurring transactions, added automatically, for savings plans
  • Backup and restore of your data
  • English, German, French, Italian and Spanish

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Free to use.
