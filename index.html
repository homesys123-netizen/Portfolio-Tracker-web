All your investments in one place

Portfolio Tracker follows your stocks, ETFs, crypto assets, precious metals and real estate, and the cash you hold, in one clear overview. Your portfolio stays on your phone.

  • Total value and allocation at a glance, in euro or US dollar
  • Profit and loss, and the value of your portfolio over time
  • Price charts of your shares, ETFs, crypto assets and metals
  • Real estate: follow what your property is worth, with an automatic estimate from official house price statistics or your own estimate, and see your net worth after mortgages
  • Mortgage and rental analysis: monthly payment, extra payments and the interest they save, rental yield and return on your equity
  • Dividends of your stocks and ETFs, added to your cash automatically (you can switch it off)
  • Markets: indices, bond yields and the biggest movers of the day
  • Recurring transactions, added automatically, for savings plans
  • Backup and restore of your data
  • English, German, French, Italian and Spanish

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Free to use.

The app is in testing and is not in the app stores yet.

Not investment advice

Portfolio Tracker is a tool for your own record keeping. Prices and figures come from third parties, may be delayed or wrong, and are for information only. Nothing in the app is investment, tax or legal advice.
