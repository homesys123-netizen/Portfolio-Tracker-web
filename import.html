Import trades from a spreadsheet

Already keep your buys and sells in a spreadsheet, or export them from your broker? Put them into the template, save it as a CSV file and the app adds them to the portfolio you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere: the file is read on your phone.

Download the template (portfolio-tracker-import-template.csv)

You can also save it from the app: Settings → Import trades (table) → Save template.

How it works

  1. Open the template in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or LibreOffice. One line is one buy or one sell. Lines that start with # are examples and are ignored (remove the # to use one).
  2. Save it as CSV (in Excel: File → Save As → “CSV (Comma delimited)” or “CSV UTF-8”).
  3. Get the file to your phone (cloud drive, e-mail or cable).
  4. In the app open Settings → Import trades (table), choose the portfolio and choose file to upload.
  5. The app shows a preview: how many trades will be added, how many are already in the app (they are skipped) and which lines have a problem and why. Then press Import.

The columns

  • date – the day of the trade, best as 2024-03-12; 12.3.2024 works too. A date like 3/4/2024 is refused, because it means different days in different countries.
  • type – BUY or SELL.
  • asset – STOCK (also ETFs), CRYPTO or METAL.
  • symbol – what the asset is called:
    • Stocks and ETFs: the symbol you find when you search for the asset in the app (Add holding), for example MSFT or VWCE.DE. If you are not sure, add the asset in the app and look at its card.
    • Crypto: the ticker or the name of any of the 30 biggest coins, in capital or small letters: BTC or Bitcoin, ETH or Ethereum, ADA or Cardano, and so on. (See the list below.)
    • Metals: Gold, Silver, Platinum or Palladium (or the codes XAU, XAG, XPT, XPD).
  • name – optional, shown in the list.
  • quantity – a positive number (metals in troy ounces).
  • price – the price of one unit.
  • currency – EUR or USD (empty means EUR).

The order of the columns does not matter. Decimal commas and a semicolon, comma or tab as the separator are fine.

The 30 coins you can write by ticker or name

Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), BNB, XRP, Solana (SOL), TRON (TRX), Zcash (ZEC), Hyperliquid (HYPE), Dogecoin (DOGE), Monero (XMR), Chainlink (LINK), Cardano (ADA), NEAR Protocol (NEAR), Stellar (XLM), Bitcoin Cash (BCH), Litecoin (LTC), Uniswap (UNI), Avalanche (AVAX), Sui (SUI), Toncoin (TON or GRAM), Hedera (HBAR), Shiba Inu (SHIB), Bittensor (TAO), Cronos (CRO), Quant (QNT), Ethena (ENA), Aave (AAVE), Pepe (PEPE), Polkadot (DOT), Worldcoin (WLD).

This is the list at the time of writing; coins pegged to a currency (stablecoins) are not included. A coin that is not in the list can still be imported when you write the name it has in the app's search, in small letters, with dashes instead of spaces (for example bitcoin-cash); check the price in the app afterwards.

Good to know

  • Your Cash is not changed. The file holds trades, not deposits or withdrawals.
  • Importing the same file twice is safe: trades that are already in the app are skipped.
  • The history of each asset is played again, so the average buy price and the realized profit are worked out for you. If a sale would be larger than what you hold at that moment, that asset is not imported (the app tells you).
  • The rate of the day is used for trades in US dollars.
  • Not supported yet: other currencies, dividends, splits, options, and Excel (.xlsx) files – save as CSV first.
